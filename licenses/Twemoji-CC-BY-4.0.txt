Yumi Desktop bundles the COLR/CPAL build produced by the twemoji-colr project
(https://github.com/mozilla/twemoji-colr) as desktop/src/main/resources/font/twemoji.ttf.
It is used for one thing only: drawing country flags, which Windows does not
ship a glyph for.
